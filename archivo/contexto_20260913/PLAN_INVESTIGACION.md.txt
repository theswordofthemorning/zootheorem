## The target

For T=A014682, let a(n) count classes of consecutive pairs that merge by
step n with equal odd counts. Doubling gives the limit
`phi=lim a(n)/2^n`. Our precise target is **phi=1**.

If F is the set of positive integer pairs that eventually merge in this
balanced sense, `lower natural density(F)>=phi`. Thus phi=1 implies
natural density one of F. The converse equality of densities does not
follow from an increasing union of periodic sets. Equal **finite**
classical heights additionally require convergence and control of first
arrival at 1. The historical name “Gao–Wu in density” should not conceal
these distinctions or replace comparison with the original statements.

## Ordered work and completion criteria

1. **P0 — Coherence.** Keep statements, data, scripts and the laboratory
   SQLite registry consistent. Correct the three high-stratum offsets and
   supply their direct verifier. Completed checks and remaining scope are
   recorded in [the local revision note](drafts/REVISION_20260913.md).
2. **P1 — Full return clock.** Extend the interior excursion bound to
   include k=0,1 boundary segments. Specify entrance, restart and mixed
   times separately. Completion requires a global clock bound under the
   actual conditional law, with its dependence on the start explicit.
3. **P2 — Exit window.** Write `u_tau=Pi_tau*u0+W_tau`, u0=c/9. Gacelas
   controls the product on long excursions. Seek fixed M<infinity and
   p>0 such that `P(|W_tau|<=M | tau>T(c))>=p`, uniformly on a declared
   domain, for a declared horizon of order log(2+|c|). Subtracting the
   exponentially small product failure would then give joint mass in a
   bounded exit window. Completion is a proof under this law, or an
   explicit family refuting the proposed uniformity.
4. **P3 — Starts and absorption.** Control the actual starts c_i and show
   divergence of the sum of conditional success probabilities along real
   visits. Check the strong Markov and conditional Borel–Cantelli steps
   without assuming the recurrence being sought. Only a complete chain
   of these implications would close phi=1.
5. **P4 — Publication.** Update both plans and the registry whenever a
   component closes or fails. Publish ranges and proof dependencies,
   distinguishing exact enumeration, written proof and formal proof.

For P2 experiments, include both signs, small starts, powers of 2 and 3
and neighbours, and starts designed by congruences. Declare the proposed
window before computing; record survivors, exits and horizon censoring
separately. A marginal median or a finite fit is not a uniform exit bound.
