## Prepared clarification for A398792 — not posted

The paper's density statement now concerns balanced merging. The live
entry's finite-height interpretation needs the same explicit hypothesis.
A concrete proposed comment is:

```text
Every counted residue class lifts to merging pairs (2^n*m+r,2^n*m+r+1) for all m >= 0. For m >= 1, neither trajectory reaches 1 before step n; if their common trajectory reaches 1, their classical total stopping times are equal. Thus a(n)/2^n gives an unconditional lower bound for the lower natural density of balanced merging pairs. Interpreting this as a bound for pairs of equal finite total stopping time requires the convergence qualification.
```

This clarifies the interpretation; it changes neither the count nor its
program. No external edit has been made as part of this audit.
